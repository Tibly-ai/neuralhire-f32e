About NeuralHire

Recruiters who read papers,
not just resumes.

NeuralHire started in 2016 when two engineering leads got tired of watching generalist recruiters send them React developers for a computer vision role. We built a firm around the opposite idea: every consultant on our team has either shipped ML systems or spent years placing people who do. We know the difference between an applied scientist and an MLOps engineer, and we screen for it before a resume ever reaches your inbox.

How we built our edge

01

Domain depth

Our consultants have backgrounds in ML engineering, data science, or technical sourcing for research labs. We can talk through a transformer architecture or a training pipeline bottleneck, so candidates trust us and hiring managers don't have to re-explain the role.

02

A track record you can check

We've filled staff and principal roles at seed-stage labs and public companies alike, from computer vision at robotics startups to LLM infrastructure at fintech firms. Ask any client for a reference and we'll send three.
