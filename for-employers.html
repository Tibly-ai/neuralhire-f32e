Process

Four steps, no black box.

You will know exactly where every requisition stands, and exactly who we are talking to on your behalf.

01

Scope the role

A 45 minute intake call with the hiring manager. We nail down leveling, stack, and the actual problem the hire will own, not a generic job description.

02

Source

We reach candidates through our own network of engineers, research alumni lists, and targeted outbound. No mass job board blasts.

03

Vet

Technical screen, take-home or pairing exercise scoped to your stack, and a systems design conversation. You receive a written scorecard, not a résumé.

04

Hire

We coordinate final interviews, help calibrate the offer against current market data, and stay involved through the first day.

What's included

Every search covers this ground.

A

Role & level calibration

We benchmark the role against current comp and title data so you're not competing against your own job posting.

B

Technical vetting pipeline

Model evaluation, MLOps, and infra-heavy roles are screened by engineers who have worked in those stacks, not generalist recruiters.

C

Niche sourcing channels

Research lab alumni lists, Kaggle community contacts, and referral chains from placed candidates feed most of our pipeline.

D

Structured interview kits

We build the systems design prompts and rubrics for your panel so every interviewer scores against the same bar.

E

Offer & negotiation support

We handle back and forth on comp, start date, and relocation so your hiring manager stays out of the negotiation.

F
