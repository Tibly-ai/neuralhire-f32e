Process

How a search runs

01

Scope the role

An intake call with the hiring manager to set a technical rubric: stack, seniority, what a strong first 90 days looks like.

02

Source and screen

Candidates come from our own network of ML practitioners, then get a technical screen run by former engineers, not generalist recruiters.

03

Deliver a slate

Three to five vetted candidates, with notes on strengths and comp expectations, usually inside two weeks of kickoff.

04

Close the hire

We handle comp benchmarking, reference checks and offer negotiation so the deal doesn't stall in the final week.
